# Conduit v0.2.0

Released 2026-11-03.

The second release, the same day as the first: seven more modules, sync
between your computers, single sign-on and passkeys, signed webhooks, phone
notifications without Google services, and an F-Droid repository.

## New

- **Seven modules** (27 in all): Kubernetes, Proxmox VE, AWS (EC2, S3,
  CloudWatch alarms), Slack, Jira, Spotify and OBS Studio. Changes preview
  first and undo where the service allows it.
- **Sync between your computers.** Keep the vault, agent rules and
  automations the same on several daemons, through your hub or directly on
  your network. Everything is encrypted with a key only your computers hold.
  Settings, Sync.
- **Single sign-on.** Teams sign in to Conduit with Google Workspace,
  Microsoft Entra, Okta, Keycloak, Authentik or any OpenID Connect provider,
  with an email-domain allow-list. Settings, Single sign-on.
- **People.** Add people, change their roles and switch accounts off from
  the new People page. Owners and admins link single sign-on to their
  account themselves; it never signs anyone in as them by email alone.
- **Passkeys on the hub.** Sign in to your hub account with a passkey.
- **Signed webhooks.** Send events to your own services, signed with
  HMAC-SHA256, with retries and a delivery log. Settings, Webhooks.
- **Phone notifications without Google services.** One tap sets up the ntfy
  app to receive approvals and alerts while Conduit is closed, with details
  hidden from servers you don't run.
- **Import from your password manager.** CSV exports from Bitwarden,
  1Password, KeePass, LastPass, Proton Pass and browsers.
- **One-line install** for Linux, macOS and Windows, with checksum checks.
- **F-Droid repository** published with each signed release.
- **Website:** a changelog and an API reference generated from the API
  specs.

## Security

Every addition went through a second independent security review before
release, and all seven of its findings are fixed here (BUILD_LOG.md,
phase 21). Highlights: single sign-on follows the provider's stable account
id, deleted items can't come back through replayed sync data, a password
reset removes hub passkeys, and the install script checks the release
signature. Creating and changing accounts is limited to signed-in people.

## Upgrade

Installed daemons update themselves once releases are signed; otherwise
replace the binary. Database changes run on start. The relay protocol is
unchanged since v0.1.0.
