# Conduit 1.0

Released 2026-11-04.

Conduit 1.0 runs everything you manage from one place: servers and
containers, clouds and home networks, code and CI, mail and calendars,
media, smart home and games. Your AI assistants work through it with rules
you set. They read freely, show you a preview before they change anything,
wait for your approval on what matters, and most changes can be undone.

## What's in 1.0

- **105 built-in modules**, from Docker, Kubernetes, Proxmox and
  TrueNAS to GitHub, Forgejo, Grafana, Cloudflare and AWS, from Proton Mail,
  Spotify and Plex to Steam and nine other game launchers, from Stripe and
  Terraform to Home Assistant, MQTT and your UPS. Any MCP server
  can also be mounted as a module, and you can write your own in any
  language.
- **AI that asks first.** Every action declares how risky it is. Agents get
  no more than the person they act for, risky actions wait for your
  approval on your phone or desktop, and one button stops every agent at
  once. Every AI you connect over MCP also gets your standards (how your
  frontends should look, how you write, what to never do) before it starts.
- **Full GitHub control by access token**, with no app to install per
  organisation, and **WireGuard networks** that reach servers on private
  networks without touching your computer's network settings.
- **A game library** for Steam, Epic, GOG, Riot, Battle.net, EA, Ubisoft,
  Xbox, itch.io and Lutris, with one-click play and tracked play time.
- **A module marketplace** on your hub: signed, sandboxed modules with
  ratings, reviews and moderation.
- **A vault** for every key and password, with restricted folders for
  teams, leak scanning and encrypted backups.
- **Sign-in your way:** passwords with two-factor codes, passkeys, and
  single sign-on for teams.
- **Everywhere you are:** desktop apps for Windows, macOS and Linux, an
  Android app, a browser app, a command-line tool, and end-to-end
  encrypted remote access through your hub.
- **English and German**, in the app and on the website.

## A stable foundation

From 1.0 on, the daemon and hub APIs, the module SDK, webhooks, the CLI
and the data folder stay compatible within version 1. See
[docs/api/stability.md](../api/stability.md). Every release is signed, and
installed apps update themselves.

## Upgrading

From 0.x, install 1.0 over it: your data folder is upgraded on start (this
is tested from every earlier release). If you installed an unsigned 0.x
build, install 1.0 by hand once; from then on updates arrive on their own.
