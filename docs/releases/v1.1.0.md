# Conduit 1.1

Released 2026-11-04.

Conduit 1.1 more than triples what you can run from one place: **325
built-in modules**, up from 105. It installs over 1.0 and keeps your
setup; the one change you may notice is that money and send actions now
confirm they run exactly what you approved (see Safer by default).

## Safer by default

- Actions an AI runs on your behalf see exactly what you see, including
  through the daily brief.
- Previews and errors of sensitive actions stay private in the audit log;
  only people who decide approvals see the full detail.
- Anything that posts publicly, moves money, changes access or silences
  alerts is marked destructive, so it waits for your approval unless you
  chose full autonomy.
- Refunds, cancellations, payments, order changes and newsletter sends now
  run exactly what you approved: the preview gives the values to pass
  (the order total, what was refunded, the status, or a fingerprint of the
  newsletter), and the run is refused if anything changed in between. This
  also stops a retried refund from paying out twice. Agents pick this up
  from the preview on their own.
- Home Assistant now protects locks, doors, garages, gates, alarms,
  cameras, valves and heating: switching them goes through a separate
  action that waits for your approval, shows what will happen and checks
  the result. Device names in German are recognised too.

## Upgrading

Install 1.1 over 1.0; installed apps update themselves. Your data folder
is upgraded on start, tested from every earlier release.
