# Conduit 1.1

Released 2026-11-04.

Conduit 1.1 more than doubles what you can run from one place: **241
built-in modules**, up from 105. Everything you already use keeps working
the same way; 1.1 is a drop-in update.

## New in 1.1

- **Smart home:** Philips Hue, Shelly, ESPHome, Zigbee2MQTT and Tasmota,
  with extra care around locks and garage doors.
- **Monitoring and alerts:** Zabbix, Checkmk, Netdata, Alertmanager, Loki,
  Beszel, Gatus, UptimeRobot, Healthchecks.io, Better Stack, Plausible,
  Umami and PostHog. Silences and maintenance windows ask first.
- **Your own platform:** Coolify, Dokploy, CapRover, Pterodactyl, Crafty,
  AMP, Caddy, MikroTik, NetBox, PowerDNS, Technitium, Headscale and
  NetBird.
- **Backups and identity:** restic, Kopia, Zitadel, 1Password Connect,
  Bitwarden Secrets Manager, Infisical, Doppler and SOPS.
- **Business:** Shopify, HubSpot, Paddle, Twilio, Cal.com, Lemon Squeezy,
  Zendesk, Freshdesk, Chatwoot and Zammad. Refunds, cancellations and
  messages to customers wait for your approval, and previews show
  the exact amount in the customer's currency.
- **Office:** Microsoft 365 (Outlook, calendar, Teams, OneDrive), Google
  Workspace (Gmail, Calendar, Drive), Fastmail and Dropbox. Mail and
  invitations wait for your approval.
- **Feature flags:** Unleash, Flagsmith, LaunchDarkly and GrowthBook, with
  a preview of every switch and one-click undo.
- **Work:** Asana, ClickUp, monday.com, Plane, YouTrack, BookStack,
  Outline and Vikunja.
- **Developers:** CircleCI, Buildkite, Codecov, Snyk, Drone, Elasticsearch,
  MongoDB, ClickHouse, InfluxDB and Meilisearch, with query checks that
  read your query the way the database does.
- **AI tools:** LiteLLM, OpenRouter, ComfyUI, Hugging Face and any
  OpenAI-compatible server.
- **Creators and media:** YouTube, Kick, StreamElements, Streamer.bot,
  VLC, Transmission, Deluge, SABnzbd, NZBGet, Bazarr, Kavita, Calibre-Web,
  RomM, Komga, PhotoPrism, Miniflux,
  FreshRSS and wallabag.
- **Clouds:** Azure, Google Cloud, OVHcloud, IONOS, Linode, Vultr,
  Scaleway, UpCloud and Porkbun.
- **Home networks:** OpenWrt, Omada, FRITZ!Box, ntopng and LibreNMS. Changes
  that would cut off Conduit's own connection are stopped
  before they run wherever Conduit can detect them.
- **Community:** Bluesky, Matrix, Signal, listmonk, Ghost, Rocket.Chat,
  Zulip, Discourse, Lemmy and Nextcloud Talk.

## Safer by default

- Actions an AI runs on your behalf see exactly what you see, including
  through the daily brief.
- Previews and errors of sensitive actions stay private in the audit log;
  only people who decide approvals see the full detail.
- Anything that posts publicly, moves money, changes access or silences
  alerts is marked destructive, so it waits for your approval unless you
  chose full autonomy.

## Upgrading

Install 1.1 over 1.0; installed apps update themselves. Your data folder
is upgraded on start, tested from every earlier release.
